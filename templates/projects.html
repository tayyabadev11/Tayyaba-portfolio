Job Portal

A Django website that connects employers with job seekers in a simple and organized way. Employers can create job listings with details like title, company, location, salary, and description, and manage them from a personal dashboard. Job seekers can browse and search for jobs, then apply directly by submitting a resume and cover letter, with role-based access keeping each user's experience clean and secure.
